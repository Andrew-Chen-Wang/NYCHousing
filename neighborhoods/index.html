Rent and the cost to build in 170 neighborhoods

Average rent is the mean of StreetEasy's monthly median asking rents from 2025-10 to 2026-09 [StreetEasy 2026]. Break-even is the rent a new market-rate home must charge in a 6-story, 60 units building using the 485-x exemption, under current law and with the suggested changes. Staten Island appears as one borough-wide row because StreetEasy doesn't publish its neighborhoods.

P1/P2/P3: City of Yes parking zone (none, reduced, kept). 485-x A/B: higher construction wage tiers for 150+ units. LDGMA: Lower Density Growth Management Area. MIH: Mandatory Inclusionary Housing mapped by a neighborhood rezoning. Historic: most or some lots in historic districts. Flood: much of the area is in the 1% floodplain.
