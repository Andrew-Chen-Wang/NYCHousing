Where the money goes

Every cost in a New York apartment project

A development budget has four parts: land, construction (hard costs), professional and regulatory costs (soft costs), and the cost of money and time. Nationally hard costs are about 60% and soft costs 15–30% [Vital City 2025; National Council of State Housing Agencies 2018; Fannie Mae 2019]. In New York land and time take more.
