What's different in Queens

  • Long Island City has its own parking area with no mandates; Astoria, Sunnyside and Woodside are in the Inner Transit Zone; most of eastern Queens is Zone 3, where mandates remain. [NYC Department of City Planning 2024; City Limits 2024]
  • Queens CDs 1 and 2 (Astoria, Long Island City, Sunnyside, Woodside) are 485-x Zone B. [NYC Comptroller 2026]
  • The Jamaica Neighborhood Plan (October 2025, 47–0) created the city's largest MIH area, about 230 blocks and 11,800 planned homes. [QNS 2025]
  • Many Queens Council members opposed City of Yes and promised court challenges; low-production districts here are targets of the 2025 Affordable Housing Appeals Board. [Queens Eagle 2024; NYC Charter Revision Commission 2025]
  • Rockaway, Howard Beach and parts of the north shore are floodplain. [NYC Department of City Planning 2021]
