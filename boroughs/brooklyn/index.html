What's different in Brooklyn

  • Development sites averaged a record $313 per buildable foot in the first half of 2025, with Gowanus sites from $236 to $417. [GREA 2025]
  • 485-x Zone B (Brooklyn CDs 1, 2, 6 and 8: Williamsburg, Greenpoint, Downtown, Fort Greene, Park Slope, Red Hook, Prospect Heights, Crown Heights North) carries the $63-an-hour tier for 150+ units. [NYC Comptroller 2026; Commercial Observer 2024]
  • Brooklyn Heights, Park Slope, Fort Greene, Prospect Heights and other brownstone areas are largely historic districts. [NYU Furman Center 2016]
  • Neighborhood rezonings with MIH: East New York (2016), Gowanus (2021), Atlantic Avenue (2024). Coney Island, Red Hook and the southern shore are floodplain. [NYC HPD 2016; NYC Department of City Planning 2021]
