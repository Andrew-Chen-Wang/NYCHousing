46 rules that shape what housing costs

City zoning and codes, state tax and labor law, utility rules and federal tariffs. Each entry says when and why it was adopted, what it costs, where in the city it applies, and whether the calculator suggests keeping, reforming or removing it.

“Per home” is what removing the rule alone would save on a 6-story, 60 units building in a typical Brooklyn neighborhood, with the site conditions the rule needs (a rezoning, historic district or floodplain) switched on.
